You're a data analyst at the NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission. To do your job, you have every yellow cab trip from 2023: 38 million rows, sitting on a supercomputer at TACC.

You will analyze all of it in pandas, in a Jupyter notebook, from your browser. You will not open a terminal, install anything, or type a single SSH command.

## [Lab](lab.html)

The in-class follow-along. Get a notebook running on the supercomputer, pull the lab files into your own folder, and work through the analysis together. Start here.

## [Homework](homework.html)

Your graded assignment. The commission has a new question:

> **Should airport fares be priced differently from non-airport fares?**

The prompt, sub-questions to guide your analysis, what to submit, and how you are graded.

## Before you start

You need:

- A TACC account with access to Vista ([request one here](https://morehouse-supercomputing.github.io/))
- A web browser
- The pandas basics we covered in class

You do not need to install Python, download the data, or set anything up. The data is already on Vista and the notebook runs there.
